I am pleased to write this letter in support of Liu Zihao in his application for a master's programme. I have known him since his first year of undergraduate study at Yunnan University. I am currently a Professor at the School of Life Sciences, Yunnan University, where I have been teaching and conducting research since 1995, and I also serve as Associate Dean responsible for undergraduate education.

I first met Zihao when I taught the compulsory Botany laboratory course during his freshman year. This course requires students not only to understand basic plant morphology and taxonomy, but also to apply theoretical knowledge through practical observation, specimen identification, and field-based reasoning. During the course, Zihao showed careful preparation, a good understanding of fundamental concepts, and a serious attitude toward experimental work. He completed the course with a final score above 90, which reflected excellent examination performance and consistent engagement throughout the semester.

Beyond classroom performance, I had the opportunity to work more closely with Zihao during a professional field practice that I organized and supervised: a comprehensive survey of understory vegetation types and growth conditions in abandoned land at approximately 2,800 meters altitude in the Jiaozi Snow Mountain area. This fieldwork required students to adapt to complex natural conditions, conduct systematic observations, and record ecological data in a rigorous manner. During this practice, Zihao proved to be reliable in carrying out assigned tasks, attentive to data accuracy, and capable of linking field observations with concepts learned in coursework.

As the Associate Dean overseeing undergraduate teaching, I also interacted with Zihao in an academic advising context. Based on my understanding of his academic interests and learning approach, I provided guidance on the selection of a suitable supervisor for his undergraduate thesis. In these discussions, Zihao handled academic planning with seriousness and patience, rather than making decisions hastily.

In my view, Liu Zihao is a student who may not actively seek attention, but who engages in learning with discipline, persistence, and respect for academic standards. These qualities are very important for postgraduate study, especially in structured and demanding learning environments. I believe that Liu Zihao will adapt well to the academic expectations of a master's programme and will benefit from its structured training. 

\textbf{I strongly recommend him for admission}. If you require any further information, please feel free to contact me.
